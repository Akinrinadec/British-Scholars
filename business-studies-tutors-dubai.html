What to include in a Business tuition enquiry

Clear information about the course and the student’s written work makes tutor matching more useful.

01

Exact qualification

Share the curriculum, examination board, specification and level being studied.

02

Current performance

Include recent grades and whether the difficulty is knowledge, application, analysis, evaluation or timing.

03

Topics causing difficulty

For example finance, marketing, operations, people, strategy, external influences or business growth.

04

Written evidence

A recent case-study response or mock paper can show whether the student is answering the actual question.

05

Assessment dates

Tell us the dates for mocks, internal assessments or final exams and the amount of content still to cover.

06

Preferred lesson format

Include location, availability and whether online, in-home or flexible tuition is preferred.

From theory to judgement

Case-study application separates stronger answers

A student may remember business terms but still produce generic answers. Stronger responses select relevant knowledge, apply it to the facts in the case, explain the likely effect and weigh the decision against realistic alternatives.

A tutor can help the student understand what different command words require, use calculations as evidence and build a clear line of reasoning rather than listing disconnected points.

Knowledge

Use business terminology and concepts accurately without relying on memorised paragraphs.

Application

Refer directly to the business, market, figures and constraints described in the case.

Analysis

Explain how and why a decision may affect costs, revenue, people, operations or strategy.

Evaluation

Balance competing factors, recognise uncertainty and reach a justified recommendation.

Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

Which Business qualifications can families enquire about?

Families can request support for GCSE, IGCSE, A Level and IB Business courses. Include the exact board, specification and level so relevant tutor experience can be considered.

Can tuition help with case-study questions?

Yes. A tutor can help the student select relevant evidence, apply course knowledge to the organisation and develop analytical and evaluative answers.

Can a tutor help with business calculations?

Yes. Lessons can cover the quantitative skills required by the course and, importantly, how to interpret figures within a business decision.

Can lessons focus on exam technique?

Yes. Board-specific questions can be used to practise command words, planning, timing and the depth required for different mark allocations.

Is online Business tuition available?

Yes. Families can request online, in-home or flexible tuition. In-person availability depends on location and timetable.
