Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

Which A Level Biology specifications can be requested?

Families can request tutors with relevant experience of Cambridge International, Pearson Edexcel, AQA, OxfordAQA and other courses, subject to availability.

Can tuition improve longer written answers?

Yes. A tutor can work on command words, selecting relevant knowledge, sequencing explanations and using precise biological terminology.

Can lessons cover required practical skills?

Tuition can teach planning, variables, data analysis, statistics, uncertainty and evaluation using suitable practice material.

Can A Level Biology tuition be online?

Yes. Online or in-person support may be requested, depending on tutor suitability, location and timetable.
