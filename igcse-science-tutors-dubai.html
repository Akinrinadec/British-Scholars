Focused academic support

What IGCSE Science tuition can address

Good tuition targets the concepts, calculations and assessment skills that are holding the student back.

01

Scientific knowledge

Close gaps in the exact Biology, Chemistry or Physics topics required by the student’s specification.

02

Scientific calculations

Practise equations, units, significant figures, graphs and multi-step quantitative questions.

03

Application questions

Move beyond memorising facts and apply knowledge to unfamiliar contexts and data.

04

Practical skills

Identify variables, interpret methods and data, evaluate evidence and suggest valid improvements.

05

Past-paper practice

Use board-relevant questions and mark schemes to identify repeated mistakes and improve accuracy.

06

Exam technique

Use specification language, command words and efficient working to turn knowledge into marks.

Why precise matching matters

IGCSE Science is not one identical course

Cambridge IGCSE, Pearson Edexcel International GCSE and other courses vary in content, terminology and assessment. Some students take three separate sciences while others follow double-award or coordinated routes, so the enquiry must identify the course clearly.

Share the student’s board, route, current grade, target, recent feedback, assessment dates and preferred lesson format before we review the network.

Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

Can I request one tutor for all three sciences?

You can request combined support, but the best arrangement depends on the level required and the tutor’s subject coverage. We may recommend a specialist for particular needs.

Do you cover Cambridge and Edexcel International GCSE?

Families can request relevant experience of Cambridge, Pearson Edexcel and other specifications, subject to tutor availability.

Can I request a specialist Biology, Chemistry or Physics tutor?

Yes. If one science is the main concern, tell us which subject, examination board and topics are causing difficulty so we can consider specialist experience.

Can tuition help with alternative-to-practical papers?

A tutor can teach experimental planning, variables, data interpretation and evaluation for the relevant specification.

Are online and in-person lessons available?

Both formats can be requested. In-person availability depends on location, timetable and suitable subject coverage.
