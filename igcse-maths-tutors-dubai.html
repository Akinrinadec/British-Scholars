Targeted preparation

What tuition can focus on

A good starting point is the exact specification, current grade, target and the topics where marks are being lost.

01

Number and algebra

Develop fluency with calculations, equations, inequalities, sequences and algebraic manipulation.

02

Graphs and functions

Connect equations to graphical representations and interpret gradients, intersections and transformations.

03

Geometry and trigonometry

Strengthen reasoning with angles, similarity, vectors, measures and trigonometric relationships.

04

Statistics and probability

Work accurately with data, averages, diagrams, probability and the interpretation of results.

05

Exam technique

Practise selecting methods, showing complete working, checking answers and managing time.

06

Confidence and gaps

Identify missing foundations before moving into more demanding examination questions.

Course-specific matching

The examination board matters

IGCSE Maths courses cover many common ideas, but the structure, terminology, calculator rules and style of assessment can differ. Sharing the exact board and syllabus helps us consider tutors who understand what the student is preparing for.

We also ask whether the immediate priority is closing knowledge gaps, moving towards a target grade or preparing efficiently for mocks and final examinations.

Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

Which IGCSE Maths examination boards can you support?

Families can request tutors with Cambridge, Pearson Edexcel and other relevant examination-board experience. Include the exact specification where known.

Can a tutor help with Core or Extended Maths?

Yes. Tell us which route the student is following, their current level and target so we can consider tutors with suitable experience.

Is online IGCSE Maths tuition available?

Yes. Families can request online, in-home or flexible tuition. In-person availability depends on location and timetable.

Can tuition focus on exam technique?

Yes. A tutor can work on interpreting questions, showing complete working, managing time and reviewing errors using appropriate practice material.
