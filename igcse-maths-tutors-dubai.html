Before we match a tutor

Five details that make the enquiry more useful

The more specific the starting information, the easier it is to consider a tutor whose experience fits the student rather than simply finding someone who teaches Maths.

01

Examination board

Share Cambridge, Pearson Edexcel or another board, plus the syllabus or specification code if you know it.

02

Tier or route

For example, Cambridge Core or Extended, or Pearson Edexcel Foundation or Higher.

03

Current and target grade

This helps distinguish between rebuilding foundations, securing a pass, or pushing towards the highest grades.

04

Tell us the topics, question types or paper sections that repeatedly cause difficulty.

05

Exam timeline

06

Board-specific preparation

Cambridge and Pearson Edexcel are not interchangeable

Students may study very similar mathematical ideas across IGCSE courses, but the terminology, tier structure, calculator expectations, assessment objectives and style of questions can differ. That is why we ask for the exact course before making an introduction.

For Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580, families should tell us whether the student is following Core or Extended. For Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Mathematics A 4MA1, Foundation or Higher is relevant. Cambridge International Mathematics 0607 has its own structure and should also be identified separately.

Cambridge 0580

Support can be aligned to the student’s Core or Extended route and the style of questions used in that syllabus.

Cambridge 0607

Where a student follows International Mathematics, we look for relevant experience rather than assuming it is the same as 0580.

Pearson Edexcel 4MA1

Foundation or Higher tier, specification content and paper practice can be considered when matching.

Other specifications

If the school uses another board, include the precise qualification so we can review suitable tutor experience.

Targeted preparation

What tuition can focus on

The goal is not simply to complete more questions. A tutor can identify why marks are being lost and work on the underlying knowledge, method and exam habits.

01

Number and algebra

Develop fluency with calculations, equations, inequalities, sequences, proportional reasoning and algebraic manipulation.

02

Graphs and functions

Connect equations to graphical representations and interpret gradients, intersections and transformations accurately.

03

Geometry and trigonometry

Strengthen reasoning with angles, similarity, vectors, measures and trigonometric relationships.

04

Statistics and probability

Work accurately with data, averages, diagrams, probability and the interpretation of results.

05

Multi-step problems

Learn how to identify the information that matters, choose a method and connect several mathematical steps without losing accuracy.

06

Exam technique

Improve complete working, calculator use, checking, time management and the interpretation of unfamiliar questions.

Past papers with a purpose

Practice should diagnose, not just repeat

Past papers are most useful when the student and tutor review why an answer went wrong. Was the underlying topic not understood? Was the correct method known but applied inaccurately? Was important information missed in the wording? Or did time pressure lead to an incomplete solution?

Board-specific questions and mark schemes can then be used to practise the exact skill that needs improving. That is more useful than working through large numbers of unrelated questions without analysing the errors.

Knowledge gap

Return to the concept and rebuild the missing foundation before increasing difficulty.

Method selection

Practise recognising which mathematical approach a question is testing.

Incomplete working

Develop clearer written methods so marks are not lost unnecessarily.

Timing and accuracy

Use timed sections when appropriate, followed by careful error analysis rather than simply recording a score.

Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

Which IGCSE Maths examination boards can you support?

Families can request tutors with Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580, Cambridge International Mathematics 0607, Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Mathematics A 4MA1 and other relevant examination-board experience, subject to tutor availability.

Can a tutor help with Core, Extended, Foundation or Higher Maths?

Yes. Tell us the exact board and route. Cambridge 0580 uses Core and Extended, while Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Mathematics A uses Foundation and Higher tiers.

Can tuition use past papers and mark schemes?

Yes. Board-specific past-paper questions and mark schemes can be used appropriately to identify gaps, improve method selection and develop exam technique.

What should I send when making an enquiry?

The most useful details are the examination board and specification, tier or route, current and target grade, topics causing difficulty, mock or exam date and preferred lesson format.

Is online IGCSE Maths tuition available?

Yes. Families can request online, in-home or flexible tuition. In-person availability depends on location and timetable.
