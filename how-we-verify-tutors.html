Focused academic support

A careful, practical review

Verification is not a single badge. It is a process of reviewing the information relevant to a tutor’s professional background and the family’s request.

01

Identity information

We review submitted identity information so the educator can be connected to the details provided.

02

Teaching qualifications

Relevant teaching qualifications and certificates are reviewed where submitted.

03

Academic credentials

Subject qualifications are considered in relation to the level and support requested.

04

Curriculum experience

We look at experience with British and international programmes such as IGCSE, A Level and IB.

05

Teaching history

Professional experience helps us understand the ages, subjects and settings in which an educator has worked.

06

Family requirements

A verified educator must still be a suitable match for the subject, level, location, format and teaching need.

A considered recommendation

We look beyond the subject name

Verification helps establish an educator’s background, but it does not mean every verified tutor is right for every student. Subject knowledge, teaching approach, availability and the child’s particular needs still matter.

British Scholars therefore combines document review with personal matching. We use the information provided by the family to identify educators whose experience is relevant to the request.

Relevant curriculum experience

We consider whether the educator’s experience matches the programme, level and subject requested.

Qualifications and identity reviewed

Submitted qualifications, certification and identity information are reviewed as part of our process.

Practical availability

Recommendations take account of location, online or in-person preference and schedule.

Human support

Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

Does British Scholars review teaching qualifications?

Yes. Relevant teaching qualifications and certificates submitted by educators are reviewed as part of the registration process.

Do you review tutor identity?

Identity information is reviewed as part of connecting the individual to the professional information submitted.

Does verification guarantee a particular result?

No tutor or service can guarantee academic results. Progress depends on factors including attendance, effort, starting point, teaching fit and the time available.

Are all verified tutors suitable for every student?

No. Verification is only one part of the process. We also consider subject, level, curriculum experience, teaching approach, location and availability.

Can parents speak to a tutor before deciding?

Parents can discuss the proposed support with an introduced tutor and decide whether they wish to proceed.
