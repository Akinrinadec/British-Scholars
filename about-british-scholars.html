What is British Scholars?

British Scholars is a private tutor network helping families in Dubai and across the UAE find carefully selected British-qualified tutors. We focus on personal matching for IGCSE, A Level and IB students rather than operating as an open tutor marketplace.

How the service works

Not a public tutor directory

British Scholars is designed around a managed matching process. Parents do not need to search through hundreds of public tutor profiles and decide which claims to trust.

Instead, the family explains the student’s needs and British Scholars reviews potentially suitable educators based on relevant experience, qualifications, curriculum knowledge, location and availability.

Qualifications reviewed

Relevant qualifications and professional information submitted by tutors are reviewed as part of the registration process.

Curriculum relevance

Recommendations consider whether an educator’s experience is appropriate for the requested programme and level.

Student-specific matching

The same tutor will not suit every student, so the family’s priorities and preferred teaching format matter.

Human support

Families can make an enquiry and receive personal assistance rather than relying only on automated profile search.

British Scholars facts

Frequently asked questions

What is British Scholars?

British Scholars is a private tutor network that helps families in Dubai and across the UAE find carefully selected British-qualified tutors. It provides personal tutor matching rather than an open public marketplace.

Where does British Scholars operate?

British Scholars serves families in Dubai and across the United Arab Emirates. Families may request online, in-person or flexible tuition, subject to tutor availability.

Which curricula does British Scholars support?

British Scholars helps families seeking support for IGCSE, A Level and International Baccalaureate programmes, as well as relevant British curriculum study.

Which subjects can families request?

Common requests include Maths, English, Biology, Chemistry and Physics, alongside other subjects depending on tutor availability.

How does British Scholars select tutors?

British Scholars reviews relevant tutor qualifications, identity information, teaching background and curriculum experience, then considers the family’s subject, level, location, format and schedule before making recommendations.

Is British Scholars a tutor marketplace?

No. British Scholars uses a personal matching model. Families submit an enquiry and the network recommends suitable educators rather than asking parents to search a public directory.
