What tuition can address

Good tuition targets the concepts and assessment skills that are holding the student back.

01

Models of matter

Connect atomic structure, bonding and intermolecular forces to physical and chemical properties.

02

Counting particles

Strengthen mole calculations, stoichiometry, gases, solutions and quantitative reasoning.

03

Reactivity and energetics

Understand why reactions occur and apply energy, entropy and equilibrium ideas accurately.

04

Acids and bases

Work confidently with pH, equilibria, buffers, titrations and the relevant calculations.

05

Organic chemistry

Recognise structures, mechanisms, functional-group reactions and spectroscopic evidence.

06

Practical and exam skills

Interpret data, evaluate methods and communicate explanations with precise chemical language.

Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

Can I request an IB Chemistry HL tutor?

Yes. State SL or HL and list the topics or assessment areas needing attention.

Can a tutor help with Chemistry calculations?

Yes. Tuition can target moles, energetics, equilibrium, acids and bases, kinetics and other quantitative parts of the course.

Can tuition help with the Internal Assessment?

A tutor may teach relevant skills and discuss general planning or feedback, but assessed work must remain entirely the student’s own.

Is online IB Chemistry tuition available?

Online or in-person support can be requested. Availability depends on timetable, location and suitable tutor experience.
