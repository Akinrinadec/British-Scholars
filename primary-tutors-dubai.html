What to include in a primary tuition enquiry

Primary learning is cumulative. Clear information about the child’s school programme and present needs helps us consider an appropriate tutor.

01

Year group and school

Tell us the child’s year group and the curriculum followed by the school.

02

Specific focus

For example phonics, reading fluency, comprehension, spelling, writing, arithmetic or problem solving.

03

Teacher feedback

Recent reports or work samples can help identify whether the priority is confidence, consolidation or extension.

04

Learning preferences

Share what helps the child engage, as well as any approaches that have not worked well.

05

Practical details

Include location, preferred days, lesson format and whether a parent will be present.

06

Short-term goal

A clear initial goal makes early progress easier to review with the tutor.

KS1 and KS2 foundations

Support that fits how younger children learn

Primary tuition should be age-appropriate, focused and encouraging. A suitable tutor can break learning into manageable steps, check understanding through practice and help the child explain their thinking.

The exact content should follow the pupil’s year group and school curriculum. Families can request help with one priority or a balanced programme across English and Maths.

Reading and phonics

Build decoding, fluency, vocabulary and confident reading at an appropriate level.

Writing

Develop sentence control, spelling, punctuation, organisation and purposeful vocabulary.

Number

Strengthen place value, calculation fluency, fractions and other year-group foundations.

Reasoning

Help the child explain methods, select useful information and approach unfamiliar problems.

A considered match

Experience and rapport both matter

For younger learners, clear communication with the family and a positive working relationship are especially important.

Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

Which primary year groups can families enquire about?

Families can submit enquiries for Key Stage 1 and Key Stage 2 pupils. Tell us the exact year group, school curriculum and current priority so relevant tutor experience can be considered.

Can a tutor support both English and Maths?

Families may request support in one subject or across both. The proposed focus depends on the child’s needs, the tutor’s experience and the time available.

Can tuition help with reading and phonics?

Yes. Include the child’s year group, current reading level if known, school feedback and the specific difficulty so an appropriate match can be considered.

How do you match a primary tutor?

We consider relevant qualifications and experience alongside the child’s year group, learning needs, location, timetable, preferred format and the importance of a positive working relationship.

Is in-home primary tuition available in Dubai?

Families can request in-home, online or flexible tuition. In-person availability depends on location, safeguarding arrangements and tutor timetable.
