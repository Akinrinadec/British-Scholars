Focused academic support

Where an IB tutor can help

IB students often need more than extra practice. They need support that reflects the syllabus, assessment style and pace of their programme.

01

Subject understanding

Work through difficult concepts, close knowledge gaps and build a firmer foundation before moving on to more demanding material.

02

Exam preparation

Practise applying knowledge under exam conditions, improve the structure of answers and focus revision on areas that need the most attention.

03

Coursework guidance

Help students understand expectations, organise their work and develop independent approaches without completing assessed work for them.

04

Higher Level support

Targeted tuition for students managing the additional depth, pace and workload of an IB Higher Level subject.

05

Confidence and routine

Create a clear study rhythm and give students space to ask questions they may not raise in a busy classroom.

06

Online or in person

Request online tuition, lessons at home in Dubai or a flexible combination depending on subject and tutor availability.

A considered recommendation

We look beyond the subject name

Two students taking the same IB subject may need completely different support. One may be struggling with underlying concepts, while another understands the material but needs help applying it in assessments.

That is why we ask about the programme, level, goals, location, preferred format and any specific concerns before reviewing suitable tutors.

Questions from parents

IB tuition FAQs

Can you help with IB Diploma subjects?

Yes. We can help families find tutors for individual Diploma Programme subjects, including Higher Level and Standard Level support, subject to tutor availability.

Do you support MYP students?

Yes. Include the student’s year group, subject and school programme in the enquiry so we can review tutors with relevant experience.

Is tuition available online and in person?

You can request online, in-home or flexible tuition. In-person availability will depend on your Dubai location, the subject and the preferred schedule.

Can a tutor complete coursework or an Internal Assessment?

No. A tutor can explain expectations, teach the underlying skills and provide appropriate academic guidance, but assessed work must remain the student’s own.

How are IB tutors selected?

We review qualifications, identity, relevant teaching experience and curriculum experience before making a recommendation. We also consider the needs and practical preferences included in your enquiry.
