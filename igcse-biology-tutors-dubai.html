Common areas for IGCSE Biology tuition

The exact specification matters, but these are frequent areas where students need stronger understanding or application.

01

Cells and organisation

Build clear understanding of cell structure, enzymes, transport and how biological organisation links across scales.

02

Human biology

Strengthen knowledge of nutrition, respiration, circulation, coordination and homeostasis.

03

Inheritance and variation

Practise genetics, reproduction, variation, selection and interpreting inheritance questions accurately.

04

Ecology

Connect food webs, nutrient cycles, populations, human impacts and evidence from ecological data.

05

Practical and data skills

Work on variables, graph interpretation, experimental design, evaluation and conclusions.

06

Exam technique

Use command words carefully, apply knowledge to new contexts and produce precise mark-scheme language.

Why course matching matters

Biology specifications are not identical

Cambridge IGCSE and Pearson Edexcel International GCSE differ in structure, emphasis and assessment style. A tutor who knows the student's actual course can choose more relevant examples and past-paper questions.

When you enquire, include the board, current grade or working level, target, recent test feedback, difficult topics and examination dates.
