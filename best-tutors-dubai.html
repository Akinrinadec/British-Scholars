Questions from parents

Choosing the right tutor

Who are the best tutors in Dubai for my child?

The best fit depends on the exact curriculum, subject, current attainment, learning needs and timetable. Ask for evidence of relevant teaching experience and agree how progress will be reviewed rather than relying on a general ranking.

Should I choose a tutor-matching service, a centre or an independent tutor?

Compare how each option selects the tutor, organises lessons, handles changes and reports progress. Confirm whether lessons are individual or in a group, and ask for the full costs and terms before deciding.

How does British Scholars help parents choose?

British Scholars reviews the family’s request and considers tutors by curriculum, subject, level, learning priorities and availability. Submitted qualifications and identity information are reviewed; parents can read the verification process and ask what has been checked for a proposed tutor.

What should I compare besides the hourly rate?

Compare lesson duration, one-to-one or group format, preparation, feedback, travel, matching or administration fees, cancellation terms and replacement arrangements. Request a written breakdown before committing.

Does British Scholars guarantee grades or a particular tutor?

No grade outcome is guaranteed. Suitable introductions depend on the student’s needs and tutor availability. Agree goals, expectations and a review point before lessons begin.
