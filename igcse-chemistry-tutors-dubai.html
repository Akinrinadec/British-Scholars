Targeted support

Common areas for IGCSE Chemistry tuition

The course varies by board, but these are frequent areas where students lose marks.

01

Atomic structure and bonding

Understand particles, electronic structure, ionic and covalent bonding, metallic structure and how bonding affects properties.

02

Moles and chemical calculations

Practise formulae, equations, reacting masses, concentrations, gases and percentage calculations where required.

03

Acids, salts and reactivity

Build confidence with pH, neutralisation, salt preparation, redox, electrolysis and reactivity patterns.

04

Energetics and rates

Connect particle explanations to energy changes, collision theory, catalysts and factors affecting reaction rate.

05

Organic Chemistry

Organise hydrocarbons, functional groups, reactions and conditions into a clear and memorable framework.

06

Practical and exam technique

Interpret methods and data, evaluate experiments and use board-specific past questions to improve written precision.

Why course matching matters

Cambridge and Edexcel do not assess Chemistry identically

The core science overlaps, but terminology, practical assessment and question style can differ. The tutor should know the specification the student is actually preparing for.

When you enquire, include the board, current grade or working level, target, recent assessment feedback and the topics causing most difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

Do you support Cambridge and Pearson Edexcel IGCSE Chemistry?

Families can request tutors with relevant Cambridge, Pearson Edexcel and other specification experience, subject to availability.

Can tuition focus on Chemistry calculations?

Yes. Tutors can work on moles, equations, formulae, concentrations, energetics and other quantitative skills required by the student's course.

Can a tutor help with practical and exam questions?

Yes. Tuition can cover experimental methods, variables, data interpretation, evaluation, command words and board-specific past-paper practice.
