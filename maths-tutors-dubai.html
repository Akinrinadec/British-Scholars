What Maths tuition can focus on

The right starting point depends on whether the student is missing core knowledge, struggling to apply it or losing marks in examinations.

01

Number and algebra

Strengthen essential methods, manipulation and fluency so later topics become more manageable.

02

Geometry and measures

Build confidence with diagrams, properties, constructions, trigonometry and spatial reasoning.

03

Graphs and functions

Understand relationships, interpret representations and connect algebraic and graphical approaches.

04

Statistics and probability

Work confidently with data, distributions, probability and the interpretation of results.

05

Exam technique

Show working clearly, avoid preventable errors and manage time across different question types.

06

Higher-level Maths

Request specialist support for A Level, Further Maths or IB Higher Level content.

Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

Which Maths curricula do you cover?

Parents can request support for IGCSE, GCSE, A Level, IB and other British curriculum pathways. Include the course and examination board where known.

Can you help a student who has lost confidence in Maths?

Yes. A tutor can work at an appropriate pace, address knowledge gaps and help the student rebuild confidence through clear explanations and regular practice.

Is Further Maths tuition available?

Further Maths support may be available depending on the level, examination board and tutor availability.

Do Maths tutors provide homework?

This varies by tutor and family preference. You can request structured practice between lessons when completing additional work would be helpful.

Can lessons be provided at home in Dubai?

In-home tuition may be available depending on your location and the tutor’s schedule. Online tuition is also available.
