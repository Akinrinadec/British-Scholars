Where an IGCSE tutor can help

Effective IGCSE tuition should respond to the student’s exam board, current level and the areas that are holding back progress.

01

Subject foundations

Revisit topics that were not fully understood and build the knowledge needed for more demanding work.

02

Exam technique

Learn how to interpret questions, show working clearly and structure answers around the mark scheme.

03

Revision planning

Turn a broad syllabus into a realistic plan with clear priorities and regular practice.

04

Past-paper practice

Use relevant questions to identify patterns, improve timing and become more comfortable under exam conditions.

05

Targeted subject help

Focus lessons on specific topics rather than repeating material the student already understands.

06

Online or in person

Request online tuition, lessons at home in Dubai or a flexible combination where available.

Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

Which IGCSE examination boards do you support?

Families can request tutors with experience of Cambridge, Pearson Edexcel and other British examination boards. Include the exact board in your enquiry where known.

Can you help with individual sciences and Combined Science?

Yes. Support may be available for Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Combined Science and Coordinated Sciences, subject to tutor availability.

Is IGCSE Maths tuition available?

Yes. Tell us the examination board, current year group and the topics that need attention so we can review suitable Maths tutors.

Do tutors provide online and in-person lessons?

You can request online, in-home or flexible tuition. In-person availability depends on location, subject and timetable.

When should IGCSE tuition begin?

Students can benefit at any stage. Starting earlier allows more time to close gaps, while focused tuition closer to examinations can support revision and exam technique.
