Which tutoring subjects are Dubai parents asking for?

We reviewed the subject selections recorded in recent British Scholars parent enquiries. Maths appeared most often, with English and Science close behind.

This is an anonymised snapshot of our own enquiries. It is useful for understanding the families approaching British Scholars, but it should not be treated as a survey of the whole UAE tutoring market.

PHYSICS 2 · BIOLOGY 1

Individual sciences

Named Physics and Biology requests sit alongside the broader Science category. We clarify the exact curriculum and level before recommending an educator.

What the numbers do not show

A subject name is only the starting point

Two Maths enquiries can be completely different. One child may need primary confidence support while another needs an IB Higher Level specialist. Curriculum, examination board, year group, current concerns and teaching fit still determine the match.

How we use the information

The enquiry helps us narrow down educators with relevant subject and curriculum experience. We then consider format, location, timetable and the way the student learns before making an introduction.

Method note

How to read this snapshot

What data was used?

Anonymised subject selections available in the British Scholars parent-enquiry records on 15 November 2026. No parent or child details are included.

Does this represent all tutoring demand in Dubai?

No. It is a small operational sample from families who contacted British Scholars. It describes our current enquiry mix, not the entire Dubai or UAE market.

Will the figures change?

Yes. Demand changes through the school year and around mock and examination periods. We will date future updates so readers can see which snapshot they are using.
