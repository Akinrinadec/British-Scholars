Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

Which A Level Chemistry examination boards can you support?

Families can request tutors with experience of Pearson Edexcel, Cambridge, AQA, OxfordAQA and other relevant specifications, subject to availability.

Can a tutor help with Chemistry calculations?

Yes. Tuition can focus on moles, equations, energetics, equilibria, rates and other quantitative skills at the appropriate level.

Can tutors support required practical skills?

Tutors can teach methods, data analysis, uncertainty and evaluation, but they cannot complete assessed practical work for the student.

Is online A Level Chemistry tuition available?

Yes. Online tuition is available, and families may also request in-person support across Dubai.
