High-value problem areas

Where Chemistry students often need support

The strongest tutoring plan identifies whether marks are being lost through knowledge gaps, calculations, weak explanations or poor transfer to unfamiliar questions.

01

Moles, equations and titrations

Build reliable methods for reacting masses, concentrations, gas calculations, titration questions and multi-stage quantitative problems.

02

Energetics and equilibria

Link models to calculations involving enthalpy, rates, equilibrium, acids, bases and pH where required by the course.

03

Organic reactions and mechanisms

Organise reagents, conditions, mechanisms, synthesis routes and analytical evidence into a connected framework.

04

Atomic structure and bonding

Move beyond memorised facts by explaining periodic trends, structure, intermolecular forces and reactivity precisely.

05

Practical and data skills

Practise methods, graph interpretation, uncertainty, evaluation and the practical reasoning assessed by the specification.

06

Past-paper technique

Use board-specific questions and mark schemes to improve command words, precision, calculation layout and application.

Why board experience matters

Generic Chemistry tuition can miss the assessment

Pearson Edexcel, Cambridge International, AQA and OxfordAQA organise content and practical assessment differently. The language expected in written answers and the style of multi-step questions can also vary.

For the best match, share the board, AS or A Level stage, current grade or working level, target, recent test feedback and upcoming mock or final examination dates.

Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

Which A Level Chemistry examination boards can you support?

Families can request tutors with relevant experience of Pearson Edexcel, Cambridge International, AQA, OxfordAQA and other specifications, subject to availability.

Can tuition focus on Chemistry calculations?

Yes. Support can target moles, titrations, energetics, equilibria, rates, pH and other quantitative topics required by the student's specification.

Can tutors help with organic mechanisms and synthesis?

Yes. Tuition can focus on reaction pathways, mechanisms, conditions, reagents, analytical evidence and multi-step synthesis at the appropriate level.

Can tutors support practical and data-analysis skills?

Tutors can teach experimental methods, uncertainty, data handling and evaluation, while assessed work must remain the student's own.
