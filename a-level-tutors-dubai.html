Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

Which A Level subjects are available?

Our network includes educators across a range of subjects. Maths and the Sciences are commonly requested, but parents can enquire about any A Level subject.

Can tutors help with AS Level as well?

Yes. Include whether the student is studying AS or the full A Level and provide the examination board where possible.

Can a tutor help with coursework?

A tutor may explain requirements and teach relevant skills, but they must not produce or rewrite assessed work for the student.

Is tuition available during school holidays?

Holiday tuition may be available depending on the tutor’s schedule. Include preferred dates and frequency in the enquiry.

Do you provide online A Level tuition?

Yes. Online tuition can widen the choice of subject specialists, while in-person tuition may also be requested across Dubai.
