What Science tuition can focus on

Each Science has its own language and problem-solving demands. Subject-specific support helps students address the real source of difficulty.

01

Biology

Understand processes, use scientific vocabulary accurately and connect detail across biological systems.

02

Chemistry

Build confidence with particles, bonding, reactions, calculations and the interpretation of practical work.

03

Physics

Connect principles to equations, select appropriate methods and explain physical relationships clearly.

04

Combined Science

Balance work across the disciplines and identify which areas are limiting overall performance.

Understand methods, variables, analysis and evaluation without replacing the student’s own practical work.

Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

Can I request a tutor for one individual Science?

Yes. You can request a specialist in Biology, Chemistry or Physics, or broader support for Combined or Coordinated Sciences.

Do you cover IGCSE and IB Science?

Yes. Include the exact programme, subject and level in the enquiry so we can identify tutors with relevant curriculum experience.

Can tutors help with Science calculations?

Yes. Tutors can work on the mathematical methods used in Chemistry and Physics as well as the interpretation of data.

Can a tutor help with practical work?

A tutor can explain practical methods, variables, data handling and evaluation. They cannot complete assessed practical work for the student.

Are online Science lessons available?

Yes. Online tuition is available, and families may also request in-person tuition across Dubai.
