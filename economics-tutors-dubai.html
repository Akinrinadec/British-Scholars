Six details that improve an Economics enquiry

The same topic can be assessed differently across courses. Specific information helps us consider a more suitable match.

01

Curriculum and board

Share the exact qualification, examination board and level, including SL or HL for IB where relevant.

02

Current and target grade

This helps distinguish between rebuilding foundations, improving consistency and aiming for the highest bands.

03

Micro or macro focus

Tell us whether the priority is markets, firms, government policy, national performance, development or another area.

04

Assessment evidence

Recent essays, data-response work, mock papers and teacher feedback reveal where marks are being lost.

05

Exam timeline

Include dates for mocks, internal assessments and final examinations so priorities can be sequenced realistically.

06

Online tuition can widen specialist choice; in-home availability depends on location and timetable.

Knowledge plus application

Strong answers do more than repeat theory

Economics students often know definitions but lose marks when applying ideas to a case, selecting a suitable diagram or developing balanced evaluation. The tutor should identify which part of the reasoning is breaking down.

Course-specific questions and mark schemes can be used to develop a repeatable process: identify the issue, choose relevant theory, apply it to the context, explain the chain of reasoning and reach a supported judgement.

Economic knowledge

Build accurate understanding of concepts, terminology, relationships and policy tools.

Diagrams and calculations

Use diagrams, data and quantitative evidence accurately and explain what they show.

Application

Connect theory directly to the information, country, market or business in the question.

Analysis and evaluation

Develop logical chains of reasoning, consider limitations and reach justified conclusions.

Curriculum and assessment

Economics content overlaps across qualifications, but assessment objectives, paper formats, command words and required depth vary. The exact course should be identified before tutor matching.

Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

Which Economics qualifications can families enquire about?

Families can request support for GCSE, IGCSE, A Level and IB Economics. Include the exact examination board, specification and level so relevant tutor experience can be considered.

Can a tutor help with Economics essays?

Yes. Tuition can focus on interpreting the question, planning a relevant argument, using economic analysis, applying evidence and reaching a supported evaluation.

Can lessons cover diagrams and calculations?

Yes. A tutor can help the student select, draw and explain relevant diagrams and practise quantitative skills required by the course.

Can tuition use past papers and mark schemes?

Yes. Board-specific questions and mark schemes can help diagnose whether marks are being lost through knowledge, application, analysis, evaluation or timing.

Is online Economics tuition available?

Yes. Families can request online, in-home or flexible tuition. In-person availability depends on location and timetable.
