Why board matching matters

Physics content may overlap, but the papers do not

Cambridge International, Pearson Edexcel, AQA, OxfordAQA and other specifications differ in sequencing, practical expectations and assessment style. A tutor who knows the student’s board can use the right question types, terminology and mark-scheme expectations.

When you enquire, include the examination board, AS or A Level stage, current grade, target, difficult topics, mock or exam dates and preferred lesson format.

Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

Which A Level Physics exam boards can be requested?

Families can request relevant experience of Cambridge International, Pearson Edexcel, AQA, OxfordAQA and other specifications, subject to availability.

Can tuition address the Maths used in Physics?

Yes. Include any difficulty with algebra, trigonometry, logarithms, graphs, proportional reasoning or multi-step calculations.

Can a tutor help with practical questions?

Tuition can develop planning, measurement, uncertainty, graphing, data analysis and evaluation skills using appropriate practice tasks.

Can lessons use past papers and mark schemes?

Yes. Board-specific past-paper practice can help identify weak topics, calculation errors and gaps in written explanations.

Are online and in-person lessons available?

Both may be requested. In-person availability depends on tutor location and schedule.
