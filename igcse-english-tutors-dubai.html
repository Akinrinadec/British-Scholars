Language and Literature

Different papers require different habits

English tuition should be aligned to the assessment objectives and question types used by the student’s course. A tutor can model the thinking behind a strong answer, then guide the student towards planning and writing more independently.

Improvement often comes from making the reading process more deliberate: noticing the writer’s choices, selecting relevant evidence, explaining effects precisely and shaping an answer around the exact question.

Reading comprehension

Retrieve, infer, compare and evaluate information using evidence from the text.

Language analysis

Move beyond feature spotting to explain how choices shape meaning and response.

Literature essays

Build a clear argument, select evidence and connect detailed analysis to the whole text.

Writing

Plan purposeful responses with controlled structure, tone, vocabulary and technical accuracy.

Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

Which IGCSE English courses can families enquire about?

Families can request English Language, English Literature or combined support across relevant GCSE and International GCSE specifications. Include the examination board and specification where known.

Can a tutor help with set texts?

Yes, subject to finding a tutor with relevant experience. List every novel, play, poetry selection or other set text in the enquiry.

Can tuition improve essay writing?

A tutor can help the student plan a focused argument, select evidence, analyse language and structure, and write with greater control. The work should be aligned to the course assessment objectives.

Can lessons use past papers and mark schemes?

Yes. Board-specific questions and mark schemes can help identify gaps in reading, analysis, writing and timing when used with detailed feedback.

Is online IGCSE English tuition available?

Yes. Families can request online, in-home or flexible tuition. In-person availability depends on location and timetable.
