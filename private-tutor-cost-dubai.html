A question every parent eventually asks

How much should a private tutor cost in Dubai?

It is a fair question. Families want good support for their child, but they also need to know whether the price in front of them makes sense. The frustrating answer is that there is no single “correct” hourly rate.

A specialist supporting IB Higher Level Mathematics is offering something different from a general tutor helping a younger student rebuild confidence in school maths. The useful comparison is not simply price against price. It is price against relevance, quality and what your child actually needs.

Before comparing rates

Make sure you are comparing the same thing

  • LevelPrimary, IGCSE, A Level and IB support require different levels of subject depth.
  • ExpertiseA specialist subject teacher may charge differently from a general academic tutor.
  • FormatIn-person travel, online lessons and preparation outside the lesson can affect pricing.

What usually affects the price

There is more behind an hourly rate than the hour itself

Two tutors can quote different prices and both be reasonable. The important thing is understanding what sits behind each figure.

01

Qualifications and teaching experience

A qualified teacher with years of relevant classroom experience may charge more than a university student tutoring part-time. That does not automatically make one right and the other wrong; it depends on the support needed.

02

Subject and level

Specialist demand matters. Advanced Mathematics, Physics or Chemistry at A Level or IB can require a narrower pool of suitably experienced tutors than more general support.

03

Curriculum knowledge

A tutor who already understands the student's examination board, specification and assessment style may need less time getting up to speed and can focus lessons more precisely.

04

Travel and location

For in-person tuition, a tutor may be giving up significant time travelling across Dubai. Online lessons remove that travel but may not suit every student equally well.

05

Work outside the lesson

Some tutors prepare bespoke questions, mark work between sessions or send parents short progress notes. Others charge only for the face-to-face lesson. Ask what is included.

06

Availability and timing

Popular after-school and evening slots are limited. A highly sought-after tutor may have little availability, particularly close to examination periods.

The uncomfortable truth about “cheap” tuition

A low rate can become expensive if nothing changes

Parents sometimes stay with a tutor because the price feels reasonable, even when the child is not improving. After months of lessons, the family has spent money and the student has lost something harder to replace: time.

But expensive does not mean excellent either

A high fee can reflect genuine expertise, but it can also reflect demand, branding or availability. Do not be embarrassed to ask what makes the tutor suitable for your child's exact situation. A good professional should be able to answer without making you feel difficult for asking.

Judge value after the lessons begin

Ask whether something is changing

The goal is not to demand dramatic results after two sessions. It is to look for evidence that the tutoring relationship is moving in the right direction.

Is the student clearer?

Do they understand topics that previously felt confusing? Can they explain the method back in their own words?

Are they becoming more independent?

Good support should reduce dependence over time, not create a student who can only work when the tutor is beside them.

Is confidence becoming realistic?

The aim is not empty reassurance. It is the quieter confidence that comes from knowing what to do when a difficult question appears.

Before money changes hands

Questions worth asking

Why do tutor prices vary so much in Dubai?

Qualifications, subject scarcity, level, curriculum experience, lesson format, travel and preparation can all affect the rate. Compare what is actually being offered, not only the headline number.

Is a more expensive tutor always better?

No. A higher rate may reflect valuable expertise, but the tutor still needs to be the right academic and personal fit for the student.

What should I agree before starting?

Confirm the rate, lesson length, cancellation policy, payment arrangements, location or online format, what work outside lessons is included and how you will review whether the tuition is helping.
