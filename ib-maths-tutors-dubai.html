Focused academic support

What IB Maths tuition can address

The best support depends on the course, level, current attainment and the balance between conceptual understanding and examination performance.

01

Algebra and functions

Build fluency with models, equations, sequences, transformations and connections between representations.

02

Calculus

Strengthen understanding of limits, differentiation, integration and their applications at the appropriate level.

03

Statistics and probability

Interpret data, select suitable methods and communicate conclusions using correct mathematical reasoning.

04

Geometry and trigonometry

Develop confidence with vectors, measurement, transformations and spatial or trigonometric problems.

05

Technology and modelling

Use the permitted calculator or technology effectively while understanding the mathematics behind the output.

06

Examination technique

Practise choosing methods, communicating reasoning and managing unfamiliar multi-step questions.

A more relevant recommendation

AA, AI, SL and HL are not interchangeable

The two IB Maths routes emphasise different skills, and Higher Level work demands greater depth. A tutor suited to one pathway may not be the strongest match for another.

We ask for the exact course, current topics, assessment timetable, areas of concern and preferred lesson format before reviewing the network.

Relevant IB experience

The recommendation considers the course and level rather than Maths experience in general.

Qualifications reviewed

Submitted teaching qualifications, certification and identity information are reviewed.

Ethical assessment support

Tutors teach skills and concepts but do not produce assessed work for students.

Practical availability

Location, online preference and timetable are considered before an introduction.

Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

Can I request an IB Maths AA or AI tutor?

Yes. State whether the student takes Analysis and Approaches or Applications and Interpretation, together with the level.

Is Higher Level IB Maths support available?

Higher Level support may be available subject to tutor experience and availability. Include the exact course and topics in the enquiry.

Can a tutor help with the Internal Assessment?

A tutor may teach relevant concepts and discuss planning or feedback, but assessed work must remain entirely the student’s own.

Are online and in-person lessons available?

Families can request online, in-home or flexible tuition. In-person availability depends on location and schedule.
