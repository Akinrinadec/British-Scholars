First distinction

AA and AI are genuinely different courses

Matching a tutor simply because they teach “IB Maths” is too broad. The route and level change the emphasis of the course.

AA

Analysis and Approaches

Typically places greater emphasis on algebraic fluency, functions, mathematical reasoning, calculus and analytical techniques. The depth increases substantially at HL.

AI

Applications and Interpretation

Typically places greater emphasis on modelling, statistics, interpretation, real-world applications and effective use of technology, with greater depth at HL.

SL

Standard Level

SL still requires strong conceptual understanding, accurate calculator use and the ability to communicate mathematical reasoning clearly.

HL

Higher Level

HL requires more depth, more demanding multi-step reasoning and a tutor who is comfortable teaching the exact pathway at that level.

Focused academic support

What IB Maths tuition can address

The best support depends on the route, level, current attainment and the balance between conceptual understanding and examination performance.

01

Number, algebra and functions

Build fluency with equations, sequences, logarithms, transformations, models and connections between symbolic and graphical representations.

02

Calculus

Strengthen differentiation, integration and applications of calculus at the depth required by the student's route and level.

03

Statistics and probability

Interpret data, select suitable methods, use technology appropriately and communicate conclusions with sound mathematical reasoning.

04

Geometry and trigonometry

Develop confidence with vectors, measurement, transformations, trigonometric models and unfamiliar spatial problems.

05

Technology and modelling

Use permitted graphical-display calculator or software tools effectively without treating calculator output as a substitute for understanding.

06

Examination technique

Practise choosing methods, showing reasoning, managing command terms, using technology efficiently and handling unfamiliar multi-step questions.

Why precise matching matters

An AA SL tutor is not automatically the right match for AI HL

The routes emphasise different skills and Higher Level work demands greater depth. A tutor who understands the student's exact pathway can focus on the relevant style of reasoning, technology and assessment rather than teaching a generic version of IB Mathematics.

When you enquire, include the route, level, current working grade or predicted grade, difficult topics, next mock or final exam date and any concern about the Internal Assessment.

Internal Assessment

Support should protect academic integrity

The IB Mathematics Internal Assessment is the student's own mathematical exploration. A tutor can help the student understand relevant mathematics, discuss whether an idea is mathematically viable and explain feedback, but should not write, substantially rewrite or produce the assessed work.

Useful IA support can include

Clarifying mathematical concepts, helping the student understand appropriate notation, discussing how to test a model, explaining statistical methods and asking questions that help the student think more critically about their own work.

Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between IB Maths AA and AI?

Analysis and Approaches generally places greater emphasis on algebraic and analytical techniques, while Applications and Interpretation places greater emphasis on modelling, statistics, technology and interpretation. The exact level and student goals also matter.

Can I request an IB Maths AA or AI tutor?

Yes. State whether the student takes Analysis and Approaches or Applications and Interpretation, together with Standard or Higher Level.

Is Higher Level IB Maths support available?

Higher Level support may be available subject to tutor experience and availability. Include the exact course, current topics, working level and assessment dates in the enquiry.

Can a tutor help with the IB Maths Internal Assessment?

A tutor may teach relevant mathematics, explain expectations and give appropriate feedback, but the exploration and assessed work must remain the student's own.

Can tuition focus on IB exam technique?

Yes. Tuition can use suitable IB-style questions to practise method selection, calculator use, communication of reasoning, time management and unfamiliar multi-step problems.
