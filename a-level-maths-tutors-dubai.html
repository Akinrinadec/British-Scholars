Targeted A Level support

Where a Maths tutor can help

The most useful tuition starts with the exact specification, current working grade and a clear picture of where marks are being lost.

01

Algebra and functions

Strengthen manipulation, equations, inequalities, sequences, transformations and the algebra that underpins almost every later topic.

02

Calculus

Develop confidence with differentiation, integration, rates of change and applying calculus to unfamiliar multi-step problems.

03

Trigonometry, proof and vectors

Connect identities, equations, geometric reasoning, proof and vector methods rather than relying on memorised procedures.

04

Statistics

Work on probability, distributions, sampling, hypothesis testing and interpretation where these form part of the student's specification.

05

Mechanics

Build modelling skills across kinematics, forces, moments, projectiles and other mechanics content where applicable.

06

Past-paper performance

Use exam-board-specific questions to identify weak methods, incomplete reasoning, avoidable errors and time-management problems.

Why course matching matters

A Level Maths is not one identical course

Pearson Edexcel, AQA, OxfordAQA, Cambridge International and other specifications organise and assess Mathematics differently. The weighting of Pure Mathematics and applied content, calculator expectations, paper structure and question style can vary.

A tutor who understands the student's actual specification can spend less time on generic revision and more time on the content and assessment style that will appear in that student's papers.

Before we recommend a tutor

Five details that make the match more accurate

The more precise the enquiry, the easier it is to distinguish a general Maths tutor from someone with the right A Level experience.

01

Exam board and specification

Send the exact board and, where known, the course or specification code.

02

AS or A Level stage

Tell us whether the student is in the first or second year and whether they are taking the full qualification.

03

Current and target grade

A student trying to move from a D to a C may need a different approach from one aiming for A or A*.

04

Where marks are being lost

For example: algebraic manipulation, calculus, proof, Mechanics, Statistics, interpretation or running out of time.

05

Upcoming assessment date

Mock and final examination dates help determine whether the immediate priority should be teaching, consolidation or exam practice.

Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

Which A Level Maths exam boards can you support?

Families can request tutors with experience of Pearson Edexcel, AQA, OxfordAQA, Cambridge International and other relevant specifications, subject to tutor availability.

What information should I send when requesting a tutor?

Include the exam board, AS or full A Level stage, current working grade, target grade, difficult topics, upcoming mock or exam dates and whether the student also studies Further Maths.

Can tuition focus on Pure Maths, Statistics and Mechanics?

Yes. The exact balance depends on the specification. Sharing the board and paper or component details helps us consider tutors with the right applied and Pure Mathematics experience.

Can tuition use past papers and mark schemes?

Yes. Tutors can use appropriate exam-board-specific questions and mark schemes to diagnose where marks are being lost and practise the style of reasoning required.

Is Further Maths support available?

Further Maths support may be available. Include the examination board, current modules and topics in the parent enquiry.
