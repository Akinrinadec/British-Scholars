Where a Maths tutor can help

The most useful tuition starts with the exact specification and the topics where marks are being lost.

01

Algebra and functions

Strengthen manipulation, equations, inequalities, functions, sequences and the algebra needed throughout the course.

02

Calculus

Develop confidence with differentiation, integration and applying calculus to unfamiliar problems.

03

Trigonometry and vectors

Connect identities, equations, geometry and vector methods rather than relying on memorised steps.

04

Statistics

Work on probability, distributions, hypothesis testing and interpretation where these form part of the student's course.

05

Mechanics

Build mathematical modelling skills across kinematics, forces, moments and related mechanics topics where applicable.

06

Exam technique

Practise multi-step questions, clear working, mathematical reasoning and efficient use of past-paper questions.

Why course matching matters

A Level Maths is not one identical course

Cambridge International, Pearson Edexcel, AQA and other specifications organise and assess Mathematics differently. A tutor who understands the student's actual course can focus on the relevant content and paper style rather than teaching a generic version of A Level Maths.

When you enquire, include the examination board, AS or A Level stage, current grade or working level, target grade and the topics causing difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

Which A Level Maths exam boards can you support?

Families can request tutors with relevant experience of Cambridge International, Pearson Edexcel, AQA, OxfordAQA and other specifications, subject to tutor availability.

Can you help with Pure Maths, Statistics and Mechanics?

Yes. Tell us the student's specification and components so we can look for relevant experience across Pure Mathematics and the applicable Statistics or Mechanics content.

Is Further Maths support available?

Further Maths support may be available. Include the examination board, modules and current topics in the parent enquiry.

Can A Level Maths tuition be online?

Yes. Families can request online tuition or in-person support in Dubai, subject to tutor location and availability.
