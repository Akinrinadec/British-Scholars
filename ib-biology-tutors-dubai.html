Focused academic support

What tuition can address

Good tuition targets the concepts and assessment skills that are holding the student back.

01

Unity and diversity

Understand cells, molecules, classification, evolution and the evidence connecting biological systems.

02

Form and function

Relate biological structures to their functions from molecules and membranes to organs and organisms.

03

Interaction and interdependence

Explore ecosystems, signalling, homeostasis and relationships within living systems.

04

Continuity and change

Build confidence with genetics, inheritance, natural selection and changing populations.

05

Data analysis

Interpret unfamiliar graphs, tables, investigations and statistical evidence with appropriate conclusions.

06

Examination technique

Plan structured answers, use precise terminology and apply knowledge rather than only recalling facts.

Why precise matching matters

Subject knowledge alone is not enough

Biology can feel content-heavy, but high performance depends on applying knowledge and analysing evidence. A relevant tutor should know whether the student is at SL or HL and which concepts, command terms or assessment skills need work.

Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

Do you match IB Biology SL and HL requests?

Yes. State the level, current units and assessment goals so we can review tutors with relevant experience.

Can tuition help with data-based questions?

Yes. A tutor can practise interpreting experiments, graphs, statistics and unfamiliar biological contexts.

Can a tutor support Internal Assessment skills?

Tutors may teach methods, analysis and evaluation skills, but the student must produce all assessed work independently.

Can lessons be online or in person?

Both formats can be requested, subject to suitable tutor availability and location.
