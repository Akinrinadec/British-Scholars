Focused academic support

What English tuition can develop

Students may need support with the mechanics of writing, the interpretation of texts or the structure and depth expected in assessed responses.

01

Reading comprehension

Read closely, identify relevant evidence and explain meaning with greater accuracy.

02

Analytical writing

Move beyond retelling by building a clear argument and analysing language, structure and form.

03

Essay structure

Plan responses, organise paragraphs and maintain a focused line of reasoning from introduction to conclusion.

04

Grammar and accuracy

Improve sentence control, punctuation, spelling and the clarity of written communication.

05

Creative and transactional writing

Adapt vocabulary, tone and structure to purpose, audience and form.

06

Exam preparation

Practise timed responses and understand how assessment objectives influence marks.

Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide both English Language and Literature tutors?

Yes. Include the exact course, examination board and texts being studied so we can review tutors with appropriate experience.

Can a tutor improve essay writing?

A tutor can teach planning, paragraph structure, evidence selection, analysis and editing skills. Improvement still depends on regular student practice.

Is support available for younger secondary students?

Yes. Parents can request broader reading, comprehension, grammar and writing support before examination courses begin.

Can tutors help with coursework?

Tutors can explain requirements and develop the student’s skills, but assessed work must remain the student’s own.

Is online English tuition effective?

Online tuition can work particularly well for shared texts, written feedback and discussion. In-person tuition can also be requested.
