Targeted support

Common areas for IGCSE Physics tuition

Students often need help linking physical ideas to equations, graphs and written explanations.

01

Forces and motion

Build confidence with speed, acceleration, forces, momentum, moments and interpreting motion graphs.

02

Energy

Connect work, power, efficiency, energy stores and transfers to quantitative exam questions.

03

Electricity

Practise circuits, current, voltage, resistance, power, electrical energy and graph interpretation.

04

Waves

Strengthen understanding of wave properties, light, sound and electromagnetic radiation.

05

Thermal and atomic Physics

Work on particle models, thermal processes, radioactivity and the evidence used to explain them.

06

Equations, graphs and practical skills

Rearrange formulae, use units correctly, interpret data and practise board-specific experimental questions.

Why course matching matters

Physics assessment is more than knowing equations

Cambridge IGCSE and Pearson Edexcel International GCSE differ in content emphasis and question style. Students also need to know when an equation applies and how to communicate reasoning clearly.

When you enquire, include the board, current grade or working level, target, recent assessment feedback and the topics causing most difficulty.

Questions from parents

Frequently asked questions

Do you support Cambridge and Pearson Edexcel IGCSE Physics?

Families can request tutors with relevant Cambridge, Pearson Edexcel and other specification experience, subject to availability.

Can tuition focus on Physics calculations?

Yes. Tutors can work on equations, units, rearranging formulae, graphs and multi-step numerical questions required by the student's course.

Can a tutor help with practical and exam questions?

Yes. Tuition can cover experimental methods, graph skills, data interpretation, evaluation, command words and board-specific past-paper practice.
